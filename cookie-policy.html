Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Florida Best Realty (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. Florida Best Realty is a licensed real estate brokerage at 15807 Biscayne Blvd., Suite 213, North Miami Beach, FL 33160.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. They help a site work, remember choices, or understand how visitors use the pages.

How we use cookies

We group cookies into three categories:

  • Essential — required to store your cookie preference and operate basic site features. These do not require consent.
  • Analytics — help us understand how visitors use the site through Google Tag Manager. Loaded only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Marketing — used for advertising and remarketing if those tags are enabled in Google Tag Manager. Loaded only if you accept marketing cookies.

When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you Accept all, Reject non-essential, or open Cookie settings. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Google Consent Mode

Google Tag Manager can load on every page, but we use Google Consent Mode v2 so analytics and advertising storage stay denied until you accept those categories. Rejecting non-essential cookies still lets you use the site.

Other third parties

These are not controlled by our cookie banner:

  • Google Fonts — Inter and Sora typefaces. Google may receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • Wistia — the recruiting video player. Wistia may set its own cookies when the video plays.
  • Formspree — the callback form posts to Formspree. That is a form submission, not a banner cookie.

How to control cookies

Use the banner or in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking all cookies may affect how the site works.
